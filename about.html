Who we are

An energy developer built to be financed.

Gilgal Exploration & Allied Services Limited was incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (RC 1235316) as a dedicated energy infrastructure and EPCIC developer.

Nigeria faces a chronic electricity deficit while flaring an estimated 178 billion cubic feet of associated gas every year. Gilgal was chartered to capture those stranded flare volumes, generate reliable baseload electricity and extract clean LPG for the domestic market, pairing rigorous engineering with governance that meets the due diligence standards of international lenders, export credit agencies and development finance institutions.

Leadership & project sponsor

Engr. Harrison Odiri Ogoh

Founder, Managing Director / CEO

Engr. Ogoh founded Gilgal and leads it as Managing Director and project sponsor. With decades of hands-on civil engineering, pipeline execution and energy project development across the Niger Delta, he brings together institutional project finance, community trust and regulatory relationships.

Under his direction Gilgal set up its dual-track plan: deliver the $8.9 million fast-track 10 MW facility first for early cash flow, while syndicating the $1.5 billion flagship CCGT and LPG complex under the Electricity Act 2023.

Civil fleet & equipment

Breaking ground on day one.

Greenfield projects in the Niger Delta often lose four to six months sourcing and mobilising plant machinery. Gilgal removes that risk through a long-term lease and technical partnership with Dominant Engineering Limited, so site preparation, earthmoving, perimeter walling and turbine piling can start at financial close.

Bankability advantage

No mobilisation lag: excavators, compactors and batching plants are already leased in Delta State.
