Identity & Charter

Gilgal Exploration & Allied Services Limited was incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (RC 1235316) as a dedicated energy infrastructure and EPCIC developer. Recognizing the dual paradox of Nigeria's chronic electricity supply deficit and the severe environmental cost of burning 178 billion cubic feet of associated petroleum gas annually, the company was chartered to capture stranded flare volumes, generate reliable baseload electricity, and extract clean liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) for domestic commercialization. Gilgal couples rigorous technical engineering with uncompromised transparency and governance designed to meet the due diligence requirements of international commercial lenders, export credit agencies, and developmental finance institutions.

Civil Fleet & Equipment

A primary execution bottleneck for greenfield energy projects in the Niger Delta is the 4-to-6-month mobilization delay associated with sourcing, transporting, and hiring third-party plant machinery. Gilgal eliminates this counterparty risk through a long-term operational lease and technical partnership with Dominant Engineering Limited.

This in-house heavy civil fleet allows immediate site preparation, heavy earthmoving, perimeter reinforced security walling, and deep turbine piling to commence from Day 1 of financial close without external plant availability delays or mobilization premium costs.

Bankability Advantage

Zero mobilization lag. Critical-path civil readiness is secured through pre-leased heavy excavators, soil compactors, and batching plants stationed within Delta State.
