Executive Summary

Nigeria flares an estimated 178 billion cubic feet of associated natural gas annually, representing both a severe environmental liability and an untapped domestic energy resource. Gilgal Exploration & Allied Services Limited has structured a phased capital programme to capture this stranded gas at source, convert it into dispatchable electricity through combined-cycle generation, and simultaneously build bulk LPG storage and distribution capacity for the domestic cooking-gas market. The programme is anchored by an immediately deliverable $8.9 million fast-track facility and scales toward a flagship $1.5 billion, 250–500 MW complex with an associated 50,000–100,000 MT LPG tank farm.

Mandate

To bridge Nigeria's electricity deficit by monetizing domestic gas resources while materially reducing flare-related carbon emissions in the Niger Delta.
