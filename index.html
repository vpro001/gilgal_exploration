Executive summary

Flared gas, turned into power and cooking fuel.

Nigeria flares an estimated 178 billion cubic feet of associated natural gas annually, representing both a severe environmental liability and an untapped domestic energy resource. Gilgal has structured a phased capital programme to capture this stranded gas at source, convert it into dispatchable electricity through combined-cycle generation, and build bulk LPG storage and distribution capacity for the domestic cooking-gas market.

The programme is anchored by an immediately deliverable $8.9 million fast-track facility and scales toward a flagship $1.5 billion, 250–500 MW complex with an associated 50,000–100,000 MT LPG tank farm.

Mandate

Bridge Nigeria's electricity deficit by monetizing domestic gas, while cutting flare emissions in the Niger Delta.

What we do

Seven disciplines under one operator.

From wellhead to switchyard, Gilgal plans, engineers, builds and runs its own projects, with an in-house civil fleet for early site works.

  1. 01

    Oil & Gas Field Services

    Wellhead support, flow station maintenance, piping tie-ins and field flare measurement across Delta State.

  2. 02

    Engineering: FEED & Detailed

    Heat balance modelling, P&ID drafting, HAZOP studies and piping stress analysis for combined-cycle plants.

  3. 03

    Construction & Civil Works

  4. 04
