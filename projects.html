P-03 · LNG module

From −162 °C to the grid.

The module unloads LNG, stores it, warms it back to gas, meters it and sends it by pipeline to the CCGT plant, which feeds the national or state grid.

All nine steps

  1. Receipt & storage

    LNG carrier → unloading terminal → insulated tanks

  2. Regasification

    Transfer pumps → vaporizers → metering & pressure regulation

  3. Delivery

    Gas pipeline → CCGT plant → transmission grid
