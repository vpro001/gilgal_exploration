The flagship complex takes gas from the Niger Delta in two streams. The fuel gas stream drives modular gas turbines, and their exhaust heat runs a steam bottoming cycle. The LPG stream fills pressurized bullet storage, bottling and loading bays. Both are sized for bilateral PPAs under the Electricity Act 2023 and the Delta State Electricity Power Sector Law 2024.

03

P-03 · LNG Regasification-to-Power

LNG arrives at −162°C. The module unloads it, stores it and warms it back to gas. It then meters and regulates the gas and sends it by pipeline to the CCGT plant, which feeds the national or state grid.
