Flagship programme

The $1.5B CCGT and LPG complex

A 250 MW combined-cycle gas turbine power station, scalable to 500 MW, with an integrated 50,000–100,000 MT pressurized LPG tank farm and automated cylinder bottling in Delta State.

The plant will take 50–250 MMSCFD of flared and stranded associated gas, condition it into turbine fuel and extract LPG for the domestic cooking-gas market.

Target commercial operation

Month 31

Structured under the Electricity Act 2023 with bilateral PPAs and eligible-customer offtake.

LNG regasification module

Fuel security when pipelines are down.

An LNG import and regasification chain keeps the turbines running through upstream maintenance, from −162 °C ship unloading to grid delivery.

  1. 01

    LNG carrier (−162 °C)

    LNG arrives by sea as a cryogenic liquid.

  2. 02

    Cryogenic unloading terminal

    Unloading arms transfer cargo ashore.

  3. 03

    Insulated LNG storage tanks

    Holds LNG at cryogenic temperature.

  4. 04

    LNG transfer pumps

    Move LNG to the regasification train.

  5. 05

    Vaporizers / regasification unit

    Warm the liquid back into natural gas.

  6. 06

    Gas metering & pressure regulation

    Measures flow and sets delivery pressure.

  7. 07

    Gas pipeline

    Carries gas to the power plant.

  8. 08

    CCGT power plant

    Turns gas into electricity.

  9. 09

    National / state transmission grid

    Delivers power to customers.
