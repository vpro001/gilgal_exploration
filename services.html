Flagship $1.5B Infrastructure Complex

The Flagship programme entails the development, financing, construction, and operation of a 250 MW (scalable to 500 MW) Combined Cycle Gas Turbine (CCGT) power station coupled with an integrated 50,000–100,000 MT pressurized cryogenic LPG storage tank farm and automated cylinder bottling facility in Delta State, Nigeria.

The facility will intake between 50 and 250 MMSCFD of flared and stranded associated gas from nearby Niger Delta production hubs, conditioning the wet gas stream into commercial fuel gas for high-efficiency power turbines while extracting valuable LPG fractions for the domestic cooking market.

LNG Cryogenic Regasification Module

To guarantee non-stop fuel security in the event of upstream pipeline maintenance, Gilgal’s complex incorporates an LNG import regasification system. The 9-step Process Flow Diagram (PFD) below details the cryogenic chain from -162°C marine unloading to the high-pressure gas distribution grid.

Step 01 / -162 °C
LNG Carrier Unloading

Cryogenic marine unloading arms transfer LNG at -162°C and atmospheric pressure from offshore carriers.

Marine Jetty Interface
Step 02 / Insulated Lines
Vacuum Transfer Lines

Vacuum-jacketed cryogenic piping prevents ambient heat ingress during transit to primary onshore storage.

Cryogenic Headers
Step 03 / Full Containment
Full-Containment Tank

9% nickel steel inner tank with pre-stressed reinforced concrete outer wall for seismic and thermal protection.

Cryogenic Storage
Step 04 / Submerged Pumps
In-Tank Booster Pumps

Submerged motor pumps boost cryogenic fluid pressure to 8–10 bar and deliver to high-pressure sendout pumps.

Pump Well Module
Step 05 / 45–60 bar
Vaporization Train (SCVs)

Submerged Combustion Vaporizers (SCVs) transfer heat to vaporize cryogenic liquid back to dry natural gas.

Thermal Vaporization
Step 06 / Reliquefaction
BOG Re-Compression

Boil-Off Gas (BOG) compressors recover flash vapors and recondense them into sendout fuel with zero flaring.

Zero-Flare Loop
Step 07 / Fiscal Metering
Pressure Letdown & Meter

Fiscal gas chromatograph and multi-path ultrasonic metering station calibrate gas density and energy content.

Custody Transfer
Step 08 / Safety Odorant
Mercaptan Odorization

Automatic proportional dosing of tetrahydrothiophene (THT) odorant for leak detectability under safety standards.

Chemical Dosing
Step 09 / Grid Delivery
Gas Transmission Grid

Natural gas enters dedicated high-pressure lateral pipeline serving the on-site CCGT turbines and industrial offtakers.

CCGT Thermodynamic Proof & Simulator

In standard open-cycle gas turbine (OCGT) generation, hot exhaust gases (~540 °C) are discharged directly into the atmosphere, capping thermal efficiency at only 26.6%. Gilgal’s Combined Cycle Gas Turbine (CCGT) architecture recovers this high-grade thermal waste in a dual-pressure Heat Recovery Steam Generator (HRSG).

The superheated steam drives a dedicated Siemens SST-150 steam turbine generator, producing an additional +12.90 MW of clean electrical power without combusting an extra molecule of fuel. This boosts overall net efficiency to 48.81% — an absolute thermal gain of +22.21%.

Thermodynamic Proof
GT (25 MW) + ST (12.9 MW) = 37.9 MW

Base simple-cycle efficiency of 26.6% escalates to 48.81% in combined-cycle mode, saving ~1.7 billion SCF of natural gas annually per train.
